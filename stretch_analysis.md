# Stretch Analysis

Placing the `EntityRuler` before spaCy's statistical `ner` produced the best results on the gold subset's overlapping standard labels, improving precision/recall/F1 from `0.0492/0.6471/0.0914` in the base pipeline to `0.0590/0.7647/0.1096`, while placing the ruler after `ner` only reached `0.0511/0.6765/0.0950` because existing model spans kept priority and blocked several corrective rules. The clearest wins came from examples the base model mislabeled or missed entirely: in article `1` it tagged `Sixth Assessment Report` as `EVENT`, but the pre-NER ruler corrected it to `WORK_OF_ART`; in article `5` it missed both `Bonn Climate Change Conference` and `Paris Agreement`, and the pre-NER ruler recovered them as `EVENT` and `LAW`; and in article `7` it similarly added `Climate Ambition Summit` and `Paris Agreement`, which the post-NER ruler could not because the model had already claimed overlapping spans. The custom labels also changed the entity landscape in a useful but slightly noisy way: `POLICY` surfaced domain terms like `Loss and Damage Fund` in article `48` and `Global Stocktake` in article `170`, `REPORT` captured items like `Emissions Gap Report 2023` in article `71`, and `THRESHOLD` found climate thresholds such as `1.5 degrees Celsius` in article `1` and `2 degrees Celsius` in article `14`; however, the threshold rule also produced a partial span (`1.5 degree`) inside `1.5 degree threshold` in article `20`, showing that custom rules can add domain coverage while still needing careful boundary tuning.
